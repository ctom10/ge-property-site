About the owner · Locally owned & operated

Meet Grant Eckardt

Hi, I’m Grant Eckardt, owner of GE Property Development. I’ve always enjoyed working with my hands, building things, and seeing a project come together from start to finish. From taking shop and building trade classes through middle and high school to getting a construction management degree from SUNY Canton, I’ve always had a focus on learning and becoming a better builder.

What I love most about this business is getting to work directly with homeowners and helping turn their ideas into reality. I take a lot of pride in my work and believe that quality craftsmanship, honesty, and good communication go a long way.

My goal is simple: to provide work I’m proud to put my name on and make sure every customer feels confident and excited about the finished product.
